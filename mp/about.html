About the Conference Hall

The Sir C.V. Raman Conference Hall at Alagappa University is a modern and well-equipped venue designed to host academic and professional events such as seminars, workshops, conferences, and meetings.

Objective

The main objective of this system is to provide an easy and efficient way to book the conference hall for various departmental and academic events without scheduling conflicts.

Conference Hall Facilities

  • Fully air-conditioned environment
  • High-quality audio and sound system
  • LCD projector and presentation setup
  • Spacious seating arrangement
  • Clean and professional environment
  • High-speed internet connectivity

Online Booking System

This project includes an online conference hall booking system where users can check available dates and reserve the hall easily. The system prevents double booking and ensures proper scheduling.

Overall, this platform helps in efficient management of conference hall bookings and provides a smooth experience for users.
